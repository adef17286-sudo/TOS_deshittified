CRUNCHYROLL TOS - THE "WE SAID SO" GLOSSARY
===========================================
The obvious, boring, or filler terms that were left out of the summary.
Each one is stated in the terms so Crunchyroll can later say "it was in the ToS".
Format:  TERM -- what the ToS says about it (paraphrased)


--- WHO COUNTS AS WHAT ---

"Services" -- defined as basically everything: website, streaming, apps, products, and any "related features". Nothing is left outside the word.

"You" -- the primary account holder, plus your heirs, assigns and successors. If you sign up on someone else's behalf, you claim you can bind them, and they are bound too.

Agreeing -- happens by creating an account, clicking "I agree", or just using any Service. No signature needed.

Authorized Users -- anyone using a profile on your account. You answer for everything they do, including what they watch and buy.

Teen Profile -- for ages 13-17 only, under a parent or guardian. Not meant for under-13s. The parent promises it is only used by a 13+ minor and consents to it.

Supplemental terms -- extra rules (sweepstakes, contests, games, store sales, SMS) that win over the main Terms if the two conflict.

Incorporated by reference -- extra terms count as part of this agreement even though they are not written in it.


--- ACCOUNT STUFF NOBODY NEEDS TOLD ---

Accurate information -- you "affirm" that everything you gave them is true, complete and current, and must update it promptly. They may check it themselves or through other parties.

Password -- keep it secret. They will never ask you to reveal it to a representative.

Your activity -- you are solely responsible for everything on your account, and they may assume any message from your account was you.

Unauthorized use -- you should tell them if you suspect it, but telling them does not make you safe from being liable for their losses.

Material breach -- the label put on account sharing and exceeding device or stream limits. It is the "serious violation" wording that lets them terminate.

Sole discretion -- appears all over the terms. It means they decide, and they do not have to justify it. Used for: analyzing your account use, judging violations, trial eligibility, price errors, changing the Services.

Technical measures -- they may use technology to enforce device and stream limits.


--- MONEY WORDS ---

Fee -- the current subscription price, plus taxes and other charges for your territory.

Automatic renewal -- continues at the stated frequency until you cancel. Charged "without requiring your prior approval" where the law allows.

Payment Method -- you authorize them to charge it. They may place an authorization hold to check it works.

Uncollected amounts -- if you do not update your card or cancel in time, you are still responsible for money they could not collect.

Lack of payment -- if no valid payment method is on file when a payment is due, they may restrict, suspend or cancel.

Taxes -- usually included in the price, vary by billing address, and they may not be able to warn you about tax changes in advance.

Modified benefits -- they may add, change or remove subscription benefits. If you do not cancel, you are "deemed to have accepted" it.

Pricing error -- if the stated price is wrong in their view, they do not have to honor it, even for an accepted order. You get a refund or the chance to cancel.

Unauthorized resellers -- purchases through them are not honored and may be cancelled without notice.

Trial Subscription -- length is shown at sign-up. Needs a valid payment method. They decide who is eligible and may revoke the offer.

Promotional offers -- follow whatever terms were shown with the offer. No refunds, adjustments or price protection if a better promotion comes later.

Third-Party Subscription Provider -- Apple, carriers, TV or internet providers. They handle your billing, cancellation and refunds, not Crunchyroll.

Cancellation -- stops only future charges. Access continues to the end of the paid period. You lose any service credits, referral credits and unused benefits.

Statutory Cancellation Period -- the 14-day EU withdrawal right. Done by submitting a ticket in the Help Center.

Paused State -- starts after your current billing cycle ends. You can resume any time.
